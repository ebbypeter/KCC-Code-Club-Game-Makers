### Today's big idea

**Finishing is a skill.** Making something work for *you* is one thing. Making it work for
someone who has never seen it before is completely different — and much harder.

---

### The three debugging rules

Write these somewhere you'll remember them. They work on code, homework, and arguments.

1. **Say what you expected and what actually happened.** Half of all bugs are found in that
   one sentence.
2. **Change one thing at a time.** If you change three things and it works, you don't know which
   one fixed it.
3. **Explain it out loud.** To a friend, to a volunteer, or to a rubber duck. You'll usually
   spot it halfway through your own sentence.

### Finishing checklist

Work down this list. **Don't add anything new today** — polish what you have.

- [ ] **Instructions** — one line. "Use the arrow keys to catch the mangoes."

- [ ] **At least one sound**
- [ ] **Credits** — your name and the club
- [ ] Fixed whatever the playtest turned up
- [ ] **Shared** to the club studio

---

### Sharing safely

Your project will be visible to anyone in the world. So:

- **Don't** put your full name, your school, your address, or a photo of yourself in it.
- Comments are public. **Be kind.** If someone is unkind to you, don't argue back — report it
  and tell a grown-up.
- If you used someone else's sprite, sound or idea, **say so in your credits**. Remixing is
  encouraged on Scratch. Pretending it's all yours isn't.
