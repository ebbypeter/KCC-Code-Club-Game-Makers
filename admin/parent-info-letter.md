## KCC Code Club: Game Makers

### Ages 7–12 · 8 weeks · Starting [DATE]

Dear parents and caregivers,

The Kerala Cultural Club is starting a free coding club for children aged 7 to 12 — Game Makers,
the first class under our new KCC Code Club — and we'd love your child to join us.

Over eight weekly sessions, the children will learn the real foundations of programming — how
to give a computer instructions, how to make decisions and repeat things, how to store
information, and above all how to work out why something isn't doing what they expected. They
will do all of this by building their own arcade game, which grows a little every week. On the
last day, you're invited to come and watch them demo what they made.

No experience is needed. Most of them will never have written a line of code before, and that's
exactly who this is for.

### What we use

We teach with [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu), a free visual programming language made by
MIT and used in schools around the world. Children snap coloured blocks together instead of
typing code, which means they spend their time thinking about ideas rather than fighting with
punctuation. It runs in a web browser and it's completely free.

We'll create a Scratch account for your child through a club teacher account. We'll cover
online safety in class, and we ask the children not to put personal details in their projects.
Their work will be saved online so they can carry on at home.

### What your child needs to bring

- A laptop, if you have one they can bring. **If you don't, that's absolutely fine** — we have
  club laptops available, just tick the box on the registration form so we can plan.
- Headphones, if they have some. Not essential.
- That's it. No books, no software to install, no cost.

### What we'd love from you

**Ask them about it.** Every week they'll have a small optional task to try at home — usually
fifteen minutes, always something they can show off. There's no marking and nothing is chased.
The single most useful thing you can do is let them show you what they built and be impressed,
even when you have no idea what you're looking at. Especially then.

**Come to the showcase.** The last session is a demo evening, and it's the thing they'll
remember.

### A note on how we run it

This is volunteer-run. There are always at least two adults in the room, and we keep the group
small enough that every child gets help within a minute of putting their hand up. Nothing is
graded, there are no
tests, and we won't chase homework — the aim is that they finish the eight weeks wanting to
carry on, not that they tick every box.

If your child misses a week, don't worry. We keep a saved copy of the project at every stage,
so we can get them straight back up to speed.
