### 6. Scratch account

We will create a Scratch account for your child through the club's teacher account. Scratch is
a free educational platform run by MIT. The username will not be your child's full name.
Projects they choose to share are publicly visible on scratch.mit.edu. We teach online safety
in class and ask children not to include personal details in their work.

☐ I consent to a Scratch account being created for my child

☐ I consent to my child's projects being added to the club's public Scratch studio
