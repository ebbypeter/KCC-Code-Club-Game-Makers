# Attendance Tracker

Print this, or use `attendance-tracker.csv` in a spreadsheet. Either works; the paper one gets
used more often because it lives in the room.

## How to use it

Mark attendance as they arrive, not at the end. Takes ten seconds and you'll forget otherwise.

The column that actually matters is the one after it: **who was absent, and did you send them
the skeleton?** A missed week is harmless if they get the catch-up file, and it compounds into a
dropout if they don't. Make sending it a habit, the same evening.
